# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free and
respectful experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Graceful handling of bugs, tradeoffs, and design disagreements
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Personal attacks or harassment
- Discriminatory language or exclusionary behavior
- Sharing private information without permission
- Any conduct that could be considered hostile or unsafe

## Enforcement

Reports are handled by maintainers using:

- [SECURITY.md](.github/SECURITY.md)
- [SUPPORT.md](.github/SUPPORT.md)

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject content and behavior not aligned with this
code of conduct.
